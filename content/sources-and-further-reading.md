# Sources and Further Reading

*Digital Support and Security T Level → Sources and Further Reading*

This whole content library is a study-guide summary derived from the official specification for the T Level Technical Qualification in Digital Support and Security (Level 3). It's meant to make the qualification's content easier to browse and revise from — but for anything assessment-critical (exact wording, mark allocations, command words, administrative deadlines), always check the primary source documents below.

## Related apprenticeship standards

This Technical Qualification is aligned to Level 3 Apprenticeship standards, so its content (and the "outline content" IfATE and employer panels set for it) tracks closely with occupational competence in these apprenticeships:

- **Digital Infrastructure Engineering** (infrastructure technician)
- **Network Cabling** (network cable installer)
- **Digital Support** (digital support technician)
- **Cyber Security** (cyber security technician)

Students who progress into employment or further training can also look toward Level 4 apprenticeships as a next step — **Digital Infrastructure Engineer**, **Network Engineer**, **Applications Support Lead** and **Cyber Security Technologist** — depending on the specialism they studied and the direction they want to take.

For the full, authoritative detail on any of these standards (knowledge, skills, behaviours, and end-point assessment), search the standard title on the [Institute for Apprenticeships and Technical Education website](https://www.instituteforapprenticeships.org/).

## About this library

This is an **independent, unofficial study-guide repository**. It organises and paraphrases the official T Level specification into smaller, individually-named files and a searchable wiki-style site, to make the qualification's content easier to navigate — it is not a substitute for the specification itself and isn't produced or endorsed by Pearson or IfATE. It was built from **Version 1.1 (August 2026)** of the T Level Technical Qualification in Digital Support and Security specification; if a later version is published, check the primary source above for anything that may have changed.
